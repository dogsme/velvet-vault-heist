A top-down stealth heist in a museum gallery at night. Slip past patrolling guards and sweeping wall cameras, hide behind marble columns and display cases, grab the golden idol, and escape through the glowing skylight before the alarm meter fills.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, hold Shift to sneak, and spend your single smoke pellet (Q) to blank every vision cone for four seconds when the room turns against you. The game runs fully offline in a browser — open `game/index.html` and play.
